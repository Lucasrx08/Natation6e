## Consigne commune exacte des portraits

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 3D swimmer profile portrait for the existing Natation 6e tablet application. Reference image 1 is the exact established visual style and swimmer model to match, NOT a flat cartoon. Create ONE swimmer bust facing directly forward, shoulders and upper chest, close crop. Match the reference's polished semi-realistic educational 3D anatomy, warm skin, glossy water droplets and soft studio light, realistic face with friendly neutral expression, neat proportions. The swimming cap MUST be saturated vivid MAGENTA (#d40cd9), and ONLY the cap is magenta. Goggle frames and strap MUST be saturated vivid LIME GREEN (#72eb19), with dark blue reflective lenses; nothing else is green. The colored cap and colored goggle frames must be clearly separated and sufficiently thick to see from a distance. These marker colors will be recolored in the app while preserving shading. Plain blue swimwear, no other equipment, no raised hands, no medal, no trophy, no text, no logo, no border, no enclosing circle. The complete cap, both ears, goggles and both shoulders must be visible and centered with a small clear transparent margin. Genuine transparent background. High-detail cutout, clean alpha edges, not a screenshot.

### Portrait masculin

Subject: the same male swimmer as in the reference, young male, bare shoulders and upper chest as in the reference. Tight head-and-shoulders portrait, no lower torso.

### Portrait féminin

Subject: a female counterpart to the reference swimmer, same age and 3D character family, wearing a plain blue one-piece swimsuit visible at the shoulders. Hair tucked under the cap. Tight head-and-shoulders portrait, no lower torso.
